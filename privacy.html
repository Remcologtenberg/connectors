Privacy policy: NUCREM connectors

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

NUCREM connectors are private connectors for personal use by their owner only.

  1. Google sign-in is used only to confirm the email address of the person signing in, so each connector can check it against its allowlist. No other Google data is requested.
  2. The email address is not shared with anyone and is not used for anything else.
  3. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own server and are never sold or passed on.
  4. To have stored sign-in data removed, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
